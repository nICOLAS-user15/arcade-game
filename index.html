HOW TO PLAY

1 · Move

Steer Pac-Man through the maze. He keeps going until you pick a new direction, and the turn happens at the next opening.

↑
←↓→
Keyboard Arrow keys or WASD
Touch Swipe on the board or use the D-pad
Gamepad D-pad or left stick, Start to pause

2 · Clear the maze

Eat every pellet to finish the stage. Avoid the ghosts: one touch costs a life.

Pellet+10
Power pellet+50
Bonus fruit100 to 1000

3 · Turn the tables

A power pellet turns the ghosts blue for a few seconds. Chase them: each one eaten in a row is worth double.

4 · Know your enemies

  • BlinkyChases you head-on.
  • PinkyAmbusher: aims 4 tiles ahead of you.
  • InkyFlanker: aims 2 tiles ahead of you.
  • ClydeShy: chases from afar, flees to his corner when close.

5 · Tunnels, portals and tips

  • 🌀Side tunnels wrap you to the other edge. Ghosts slow down inside them.
  • 🌌From stage 3, corner portals teleport you diagonally across the maze.
  • ↩️You can reverse instantly in any corridor. Use it to dodge.
  • ♾️Endless mode repeats the stages, each lap a little faster.
